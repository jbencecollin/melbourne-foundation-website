Our purpose

Transformational opportunities for talented, driven students

The Melbourne Foundation provides fully-funded scholarships for secondary school children to attend a private school in Melbourne, Victoria. Scholarship recipients are chosen based on an extensive selection process designed to identify students who will benefit from, and who will make the most out of, the opportunities opened through a scholarship from the Foundation.

Through our scholarship program, The Melbourne Foundation has fostered the intellectual and cultural development of Australian school children. The Foundation is funded from contributions from its management team and the community.

Our story

How scholars are chosen

The journey to a scholarship

Our selection process is unique amongst scholarship providers. Our potential candidates must undertake a rigorous selection process comprising aptitude tests, a written application, and a series of interviews with both candidates and their families.

Stage 01

Aptitude testing

Aptitude testing designed to focus on a student's potential, rather than current academic performance.

Stage 02

Written application

A written application providing additional details about the candidate and their interests.

Stage 03

Family interview

The Melbourne Foundation meets with candidates and their families to evaluate their appropriateness for the program.
