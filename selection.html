Selection process

Looking for potential, not polish

Our selection process is unique amongst scholarship providers. Our potential candidates must undertake a rigorous selection process comprising aptitude tests, a written application, and a series of interviews with both candidates and their families.

Who can apply?

Eligibility

The Melbourne Foundation's scholarships are open to current Year 8 male and female students who live in the Melbourne metropolitan area and are currently attending public school. Scholarships are open to students whose families are unable to cover the cost of private school education.

  • Current Year 8 studentsMale and female students, for entry into Year 9.
  • Melbourne metropolitan areaStudents who live in the Melbourne metropolitan area.
  • Currently attending public schoolWhose families are unable to cover the cost of private school education.

Three stages

The path to a scholarship

01

Aptitude testing

Aptitude testing, designed to focus on a student's potential rather than current academic achievement. We believe that some students may have not had access to the same quality of education as others, and that aptitude testing strips out this effect to give a true indication of a student's intellectual potential.

02

Written application

A written application providing additional details about the candidate and their interests.

03

Family interview

As well as testing, we also conduct interviews with both the candidates and the families to ensure that each recipient has the proper support, personality, and tools to thrive in their new schooling environment.

What scholars receive

A fully-funded education

Successful candidates receive a scholarship covering entry into Year 9 at a private school in the Melbourne metropolitan area, fully funded through to graduation — along with the co-curricular life that comes with it: sport, music, leadership, camps and community programs.
