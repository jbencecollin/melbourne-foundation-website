Why donate

Your dollar goes where it matters

  • Fully tax-deductibleWe are a registered deductible gift recipient, with all donations to The Melbourne Foundation being fully tax-deductible. We will issue receipts for all donations.
  • Everyone is a volunteerThe Melbourne Foundation does not pay any wages — everyone who works for the Foundation is a volunteer.
  • Less than 5% on administrationThe Foundation spends less than 5% of its donations on general and administrative expenses, which is world-class in the philanthropic sectors.
  • A proven track recordThe performance of our past scholars has demonstrated that we have a track record of selecting outstanding, highly intelligent students.
